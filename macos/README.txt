ARSENAL / COMBAT BOT
Press P in game to arm or stop one fight. Join a world before arming.
Friends: Minecraft Workspace → Friends → enter username → Add Friend.
The bot always excludes friends. Their individual switches also protect Aim, melee and blasts.
Auto Totem runs while the bot is armed without changing your own saved switch.
Gear → choose movement, strafing, pearls, obsidian support, crystals, anchors and fire cleanup.
Put crystals, anchors, glowstone, obsidian, a sword and optional pearls / golden apples in the hotbar; keep spare totems in inventory.
Menus, detached cameras and focus loss pause combat. A confirmed death ends the fight; a totem pop does not.
Only normal server reach, cooldowns and pearl teleports are used. No win or all-server compatibility is guaranteed.
Nearby reachable fire is cleared after a defeat; lava and unreachable fire can still destroy drops.
Combat Bot always starts off after restarting or switching worlds.

CHESS.COM — AUTO NEXT GAME
Open Chess.com in the client. Choose your time and switch Auto Next Game on.
Open Chess Window for a separate board; sign in to Chess.com in that window.
For the installed iPhone Chess app on Mac, choose Connect Chess App and grant
Tung Tung Client Accessibility access when requested. Keep Chess in front.
Common native time controls are supported; use the web board for custom clocks.
For Chrome or its installed Chess.com web app, choose Add Extension, enable
Developer mode, select Load unpacked, then choose the opened chess-extension
folder. Choose Connect Chrome in Tung Tung Client. Open boards attach automatically.
Start your first game normally. After its result, the selected time is applied
and one new search starts. Pause from the board or switch Auto Next Game off.
One board follows at a time; Use This Board transfers the connection.
Minecraft does not need to be running.
This desktop update leaves the installed Minecraft core and profile unchanged.
Auto Totem includes configurable reserved-slot, reaction-delay and refill-cooldown controls.

MAP ART
Client Maps 1.3.4 stores map pixels received from each server. Map in Slot
3.5.0 renders those received maps directly inside inventory slots. Both are ON
by default under Texture Packs > Map Art and can be switched independently or
assigned their own keybinds. Their data and rendering stay client-side.
